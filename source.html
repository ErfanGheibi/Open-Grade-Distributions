Government Information (Public Access) Act 2009

Every NSW public university and government agency operates under the Government Information (Public Access) Act 2009 (NSW) — commonly called GIPA. Section 9 of the Act gives any member of the public a legally enforceable right to apply directly to an agency for government information it holds, regardless of who's asking or why. It's this right, not just the disclosure log described below, that we intend to keep relying on for future requests — see our Roadmap page for what we're asking for next.

Separately, under section 25 of the Act, every agency is required to keep a public "disclosure log" of information it has released in response to access applications, where that information could reasonably be of interest to people beyond the original requester. That's the legal mechanism that makes documents like grade-distribution breakdowns, which aren't routinely published elsewhere, publicly available at all.

The University of New South Wales

The data is currently not hosted on the disclosure log; it can be requested from gipaa@unsw.edu.au. Note that UNSW has redacted some rows using exemption clauses 3(a) and 3(b).

The data was provided to us as tables in a PDF file, but using it was non-trivial. The tables had no underlying structure — no cell boundaries or column definitions a standard extraction tool could detect — and values of zero were represented by empty space rather than being printed as "0.00%". We instead read each value's exact coordinates on the page and matched it to the column header it sat beneath, then transformed the result into a CSV (Comma Separated Values) file. We also applied several other transformations; there's more detail on the Methodology page.
