Roadmap

What we're asking for next

To advance this site, we need additional data. We will continue requesting this, but your support would strengthen our case and help ensure our requests aren't dismissed as a niche concern. Please consider completing this form — it's free and only takes a few minutes.

Comparable data from UNSW for more recent terms

We'd like access to newer data from UNSW. We'd also like to encourage UNSW to publish future data automatically and on a regular basis going forward, rather than making it available only in response to an individual access application. The information clearly isn't sensitive at an aggregate level — it's already been released once — so there's no obvious reason it should require a fresh request, and a fresh multi-month wait, every time someone wants newer data.

Data from other institutions

We'd like to expand this project to include data from other tertiary education institutions, particularly other public universities in Australia. We also hope students at universities outside Australia will be encouraged to replicate this project at their own institutions.

Access to the underlying raw data

We currently only have aggregated statistics showing the percentage of students who received each grade. We'd like access to anonymised, per-student data — for example, one record per student containing a mark and grade.

Having the additional mark (numeric) would allow us to distinguish between things like students almost getting a Credit versus students barely avoiding a Fail. It also lets us know how many students there were in total. We don't believe data of this kind poses a meaningful risk to student privacy. Providing such data would also entail lower overhead than producing aggregated statistics.

Consent-based release for redacted, small cohorts

Even in the dataset we have now, some data is redacted, and we understand that small enrolment numbers might be the reason.

Where a small cohort genuinely is the reason data is being withheld, we would like the university to make a reasonable effort to ask the affected students directly whether they consent to the release of the data. Any such request should make clear that the published data will be anonymised and explain, in plain language, exactly what information would be made public. This would allow students to make an informed decision, rather than discouraging them through an overstated or misleading concern about their privacy.

Funding

As the site grows in popularity and data, hosting costs will increase. We will never charge an access fee; instead, we rely on donations to help cover hosting costs. If you have any other ideas for funding that preserve the integrity of the project, please feel free to email me: <no-donations-for-now>.
